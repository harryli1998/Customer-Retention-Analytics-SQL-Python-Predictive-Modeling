# Business recommendations and limitations

## Proposed analytical workflow
1. Define churn and the observation window before analyzing data.
2. Compare churn by tenure, plan, and engagement level.
3. Score at-risk customers using only pre-outcome features.
4. Evaluate ranking quality, calibration, and subgroup performance on real held-out data.
5. Randomize outreach or incentives to measure incremental retention and net ROI.

## Important caveats
- The data is synthetic and results are illustrative.
- Tenure groups are not true acquisition cohorts.
- Revenue at risk is a descriptive proxy, not recoverable revenue.
- Churn prediction alone does not establish causal intervention uplift.
